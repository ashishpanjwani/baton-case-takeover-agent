## Planted problems
1. **The notice of claim gives the wrong street number.** The intake, the investigator's photos (with "212" visible above the door) and the witness all place the fall at **212 Market Street**. The notice of claim says **221 Market Street**, and the city then inspected **221** and found "No defect". Should be **conflicting**, **high priority**, flagged to the attorney. **No legal conclusion** about what the error means for the claim.
2. **The demand understates the physical therapy bill.** The demand lists Harbor Physical Therapy at **$3,150.00**; the itemized bill totals **$3,510.00** (digits transposed). So the demand's $37,880.00 total should be $38,240.00, and the memo repeats $37,880.00. Should be **conflicting**.
3. **The memo says the city acknowledged liability.** The city's letter says "This acknowledgment is not an admission of liability", and the city has not responded to the demand. Should be **conflicting**.
4. **The memo says the Medicare lien is resolved.** Medicare's letter shows $9,312.48 in conditional payments and says "This is not a final demand". Should be **conflicting**. The task to update the amount is open.
5. **No response to the demand.** It was sent 09/12 asking for a reply "within 30 days". The certified receipt was signed 09/16, a voicemail was left 09/30, the follow-up task (due 10/15) is overdue, and the client's son asked on 10/05. Should be **outstanding**, high priority.
6. **The statement-under-oath transcript was never received.** The task says Completed on 08/25/2025, the same day it was *requested*, and the memo says "transcript on file". The document index lists no transcript. Should be **unverified** or **potentially incomplete**.
