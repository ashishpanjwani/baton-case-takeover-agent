## Planted problems
1. **The client can't be reached, but his new address is in the file.** His phone has been out of service since late August, and a letter to 47 Dock Street came back "Moved, left no address". The task to locate him is open, and **the memo says "in regular contact"**. The workers' comp insurer's 09/15 letter is **addressed to 2290 Ferry Road, Apt 5** and says his address changed on 08/01. Other routes: his workers' comp lawyers (Alvarez & Kim, copied on every letter) and his sister. Should be the **top finding**, with a recovery step that uses the new address. Good lead-following: search for "address" or "Ferry".
2. **The firm wrote to the wrong scaffold company.** The client remembered "Ironline", so the letter went to Ironline Scaffold Co. Ironline replied that it has no record of the job. The site incident report, the general contractor's insurer and the OSHA summary all name **Ironside Scaffolding Inc.**, which has never been notified. Meanwhile the task "Identify scaffold company" is Completed with the note "Ironline Scaffold Co.", and the memo says "Ironline notified". Should be **conflicting** or **confirmed incomplete**, high priority.
3. **The memo says the general contractor "accepted responsibility".** Its insurer says the opposite: it tendered to Ironside and has "not accepted liability". Should be **conflicting**.
4. **Therapy ended for non-attendance; the memo says he's still in therapy.** The discharge summary shows three no-shows and a discharge on 09/05, with goals not met. Should be **conflicting**, reported **factually and without blaming the client**: the no-shows line up with his phone being disconnected.
5. **No contact with his workers' comp lawyers** about the $83,583.30 lien or about coordinating. The task is overdue. Should be **outstanding**.
6. **OSHA cited Ironside** (scanned document). The report should say this factually, note that the citation is contested, and **draw no conclusion about liability**.
