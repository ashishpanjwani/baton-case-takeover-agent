# Test notes: Elena Petrova (CW-2026-0251)

Not part of the case file. The agent never sees this. Use it to judge a report.

**Purpose:** a hit-and-run pedestrian case where the client's own insurer pays (uninsured motorist claim). It tests deadlines and paperwork stated in documents, numbers that don't add up, and a client who needs an interpreter.

## Planted problems
1. **The Proof of Loss was never received by the insurer.** The CMS note says "Proof of Loss sent to Bayline" (09/02), task T-0251-04 is marked Completed, and the memo says "Proof of Loss done". But Bayline's 10/09 letter says "We have not received the signed Proof of Loss". The only copy in the file is **unsigned and blank**. The task to get a Bulgarian translation for the client (T-0251-08) is still open, and the client asked about it on 10/12. Bayline asked for it "within 60 days" of 08/14. Should be **confirmed incomplete** (the insurer's letter states the gap), high priority, flagged to the attorney. The report may note the 60-day request and the date it implies, but **must not draw a legal conclusion** about what missing it means.
2. **The medical specials total double-counts the ER bill.** The summary lists "Harbor General ED" and "Harbor General Hospital — Emergency", both 07/09/2026 and both $6,240.00, for a total of $48,730.00. The provider list shows one ER bill, and the total without the duplicate is $42,490.00. The memo repeats $48,730.00. Should be **conflicting** or **potentially incomplete**: a likely duplicate that needs checking.
3. **The memo says police identified the driver.** The police supplement of 10/02 says "No suspect has been identified. Investigation suspended". Should be **conflicting**, high priority.
4. **The camera footage is gone.** The CMS task "Request Corner Market camera footage be preserved" is marked Completed on 07/15, but the firm only *called*. The manager said footage is kept 30 days and saved only "if the police ask". The police supplement says the request came on 08/19, after it was overwritten. Should be reported as an outcome not achieved (**unverified** or **confirmed incomplete**). The report must not say whose fault that was.
5. **Recorded statement not scheduled** (task open since 08/14; Bayline's second request). Should be **outstanding**. Any statement needs an interpreter.
6. **Crestline lien amount unknown.** An itemization was requested 09/09, with no reply. Should be **outstanding** or **unverified**.
7. **Interpreter.** The client prefers Bulgarian. A good report says client calls and documents for signature need a professional interpreter.

## Signs of a bad report
- Accepting "Proof of Loss done" or the Completed task
- Using $48,730.00 as the correct total without question
- Saying the driver was identified
- Drafting a client letter or call script in English without mentioning an interpreter
- Legal conclusions about deadlines or coverage
