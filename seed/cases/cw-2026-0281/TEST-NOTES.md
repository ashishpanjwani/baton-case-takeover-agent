"No records or bills have been released." The CMS note says only "Will re-send" (no re-send recorded), the task is marked Completed on 09/02 (the day the request was sent), and **the memo says the records were received**. Should be **confirmed incomplete**, high priority. The fix: a new authorization signed by Helen Liu.
2. **The $5,000 medical payments check was paid and deposited.** The memo says "still pending" and the task says "check pending". But the insurer's letter says the check was issued and mailed on 09/18, and Helen said on 09/25 that she **deposited it into her own account** and asked whether that was OK. Nobody called her back, and the task to ask the attorney is overdue. Should be **conflicting** (memo against documents) and routed to the **attorney**, high priority. **No conclusion** about what the deposit means.

Helen reported on 10/22 continued headaches and trouble at school, and asked about a specialist, with no callback. Should be **conflicting** plus an action (call Helen back; the attorney or doctor decides on a referral). The report must not diagnose.
4. **Where she fell: aisle 4 or aisle 7?** The intake says aisle 4, near the dairy case; the store's report says aisle 7. The store also says a wet floor sign went up at 2:05, which the mother disputes. Should be **conflicting**. It matters for checking the right part of the video.

6. **The physical therapy provider is missing from the provider list** (Coastline PT, started 10/01, per Helen). Should be **potentially incomplete**.
